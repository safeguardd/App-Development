# 🎬 My Favorite Movie: IT

Genre: Horror, Thriller  
Director:Andy Muschietti  
Based on: The novel It by Stephen King  

## 🧠 What the Movie Is About
IT follows a group of kids from the town of Derry who band together to face their worst fears when they encounter an evil entity known as Pennywise — a terrifying clown who awakens every 27 years to feed on the town’s children.

## ❤️ Why I Like It
- The story is both scary and emotional.  
- The bond between the kids (“The Losers Club”) is inspiring.  
- Pennywise is one of the most unforgettable horror villains.  

> “We all float down here.” – Pennywise 🩸
